\section{Supplementary Material}

This appendix provides reproducibility details for the controlled COCO/GQA
study. It is not required to understand the main claim, but records the data
templates, training settings, scoring rules, and confusion counts used by the
full-paper build.

\subsection{Scoring Rules}

All held-out generations are scored with a deterministic yes/no parser. Answers
whose first decisive statement is affirmative are scored as yes; answers whose
first decisive statement is negative are scored as no. Outputs without a
parseable yes/no decision, or with explicit uncertainty such as ``cannot
determine'' or ``not sure'', are counted as refusals. The same parser is used
for Base Instruct, \answerdpo{}, and \method{}.

Yes bias is the fraction of parsed predictions assigned to yes. The COCO
false-positive rate is computed over negative object-existence questions. GQA
color and left/right errors are computed over their corresponding held-out
slices.

\subsection{Reproducibility Notes}

The mixed preference data is exported from a single canonical pair file into
two response formats, which prevents accidental changes in images, questions,
or labels between \answerdpo{} and \method{}. The 200-example audit checks
chosen correctness, rejected wrongness, and evidence-hint consistency. Official
template files are copied from \texttt{cvpr-org/author-kit}; the local
\texttt{local\_xetex\_fonts.tex} shim only fixes Tectonic/XeTeX font encoding
so the official Times/Helvetica families render with proper bold shapes.
